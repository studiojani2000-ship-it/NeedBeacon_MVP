How it works

From a city name to a shortlist worth contacting.

Instead of giving you a generic database of contacts, NeedBeacon looks for evidence that a business may have a real website need.

01

Discover

Enter a city. NeedBeacon builds a pool of real restaurants with public web presence.

02

Analyze

It checks websites for visible signals such as broken or parked domains, weak calls to action, and missing useful functions.

03

Prioritize

Each business receives an Opportunity Score so stronger cases rise above weak or uncertain leads.

04

Act

For qualified leads, NeedBeacon surfaces public professional contact details when found and drafts personalized outreach around the detected problem.
